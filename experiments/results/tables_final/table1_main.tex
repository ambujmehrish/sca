\emph{Every row is measured by us in a single evaluation environment}: same VAST

(dual-encoder scoring followed by ITM cross-encoder reranking of the top~50), so the
columns are directly comparable and \textbf{bold} is the best value in each column. The

give SCA under \emph{full finetuning} -- the baselines' own regime -- as the
regime-matched comparison: 53.4 against the best full-finetuned baseline at 52.5, with the
adapter configuration adding a further 1.5. The converse check, baselines equipped with
LoRA and masked training, is Appendix~Tab.~\ref{tab:reported}: the strongest such variant
reaches 53.9, still 1.0 below SCA. HyperGRAM releases no code and its description is ambiguous
between two readings of the hyperbolic branch; the row is our best-effort reproduction of
the reading its experiments section supports, and no claim about their method is drawn
from it.}
