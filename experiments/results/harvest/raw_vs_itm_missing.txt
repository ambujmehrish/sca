GAIN (the aggregation gain, Delta_agg) is the diagnostic: an aggregator that
scores below the best modality it was built from -- negative gain -- is destroying
information. Compare an SCA cell against the released-GRAM cell for the SAME
benchmark: worse gain on equal or better single-modality features is a fusion
defect, not a representation defect.

ITM is the reported metric. If SCA leads on AGGREG and trails on ITM, the
reranking stage is where the advantage is lost instead.
