No cell was evaluated twice on one checkpoint -- no floor can be stated, and
an absent floor is not a floor of zero. Re-run one finished cell to measure it.

TAX is the diagnostic: an aggregator that scores below the best modality it was
built from is destroying information. Compare an SCA cell against the released-GRAM
cell for the SAME benchmark -- a worse tax on equal or better single-modality
features is a fusion defect, not a representation defect.

ITM is the reported metric. If SCA leads on AGGREG and trails on ITM, the
reranking stage is where the advantage is lost instead.
